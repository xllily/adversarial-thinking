# Plan Mode

Produce a robust plan, not a review disguised as one.

## Steps

1. **Separate outcome from route.** Preserve authoritative requirements and non-goals. Treat a proposed implementation as a premise unless it is itself an explicit constraint.
2. **Stress the route.** Examine only decision-relevant assumptions, dependencies, ordering, boundary states, mixed-version operation, observability, opportunity cost, and recovery. Compare a materially different route when it could change the decision; do not manufacture alternatives for their own sake.
3. **Make uncertainty executable.** Convert material assumptions into validation steps, irreversible moves into staged commitments or recovery paths, and unresolved unknowns into named gates rather than hidden caveats.
4. **Build the plan.** Give ordered actions, ownership when known, acceptance and abort criteria, and the smallest safe next step. Integrate corrections into the plan instead of stopping at criticism.

## Completion criterion

Plan mode is complete when:

- success is testable and the next executable step is clear;
- every material assumption is evidenced, testable, reversible, or guarded by a contingency;
- high-impact transitions have observable acceptance, abort, and recovery conditions;
- rejected routes have one decisive reason rather than a ceremonial comparison; and
- any unresolved choice that genuinely needs new authority or facts is surfaced precisely.

Non-material unknowns may remain. Do not block a reversible plan merely because certainty is incomplete.
