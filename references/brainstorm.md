# Brainstorm Mode

Widen the frame before narrowing the choice.

## Diverge before judging

1. Separate the underlying problem, desired outcome, and hard constraints from the first proposed solution. Record already-built work as salvage value or transition cost.
2. Cover the applicable search spaces before scoring:
   - **Premise-preserving:** improve the proposed mechanism.
   - **Adjacent:** solve the same outcome through a substitute or complement.
   - **Premise-breaking:** reframe the cause, remove the assumed mechanism, or choose a non-action.
3. Ensure at least one candidate is not descended from the initial proposal unless that premise is an authoritative requirement. Prefer distinct causal mechanisms over cosmetic variations.

Do not critique candidates until this coverage exists. A risky idea may remain useful as a probe, component, or bounded experiment.

## Converge adversarially

Compare candidates against the actual outcome, constraints, evidence, reversibility, opportunity cost, and likely failure mechanism.

Select a shortlist, recommendation, or discriminating experiment. Preserve useful parts of rejected ideas where they reduce cost without inheriting the failed premise.

## Completion criterion

Brainstorm mode is complete when:

- the option set contains meaningfully distinct mechanisms or frames;
- divergence occurred before elimination;
- convergence yields a viable shortlist, choice, or evidence-producing experiment; and
- additional ideas would merely repeat existing trade-offs.
